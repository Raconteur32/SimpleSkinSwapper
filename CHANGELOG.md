# 0.12.0

- Skins can be reordered directly on the skin wheel: press a sector, drag it outward, the wheel reflows live, click to drop it in place
- New All skins wheel option (Never / Fallback / Always), with a cap on how many all-skins wheels appear when categories have slots
- The All skins and Uncategorized views are now reorderable by drag & drop, like category views
- The delete popup always offers both actions, even on a skin's last category: remove from category, or delete everywhere
- The add-skin panel flags skins that already exist and asks for confirmation before assigning a skin to a category card
- The mod's UI chrome now ships its own textures: a resource pack can retheme the skin library without touching any vanilla asset
- Category names no longer change when the skin's global display name is edited
- Fixed the drag insertion gap opening in the wrong grid cell
- Fixed forward card drags in the library grid and the add card staying pinned
- The skin wheel keybind defaults to G
