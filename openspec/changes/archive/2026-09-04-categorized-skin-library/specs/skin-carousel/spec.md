# Skin Carousel Delta

## REMOVED Requirements

> The carousel screen is replaced by the category-based library screen (`skin-library`). Its screen-specific behaviors are removed; the culling, GUI-scale adaptation, and hover-animation behaviors are re-specified grid-adapted under `skin-library`.

### Requirement: Off-screen carousel cards are not rendered

### Requirement: Card visibility updates every frame with scroll position

### Requirement: Carousel layout adapts to GUI scale

### Requirement: Card previews animate on hover and settle back smoothly
