# Simple Skin Swapper

**Simple Skin Swapper** is a client-side Fabric mod for Minecraft 1.21.11 and 26.x that lets you switch between your local skin files on the fly, without restarting the game or navigating through external websites. Open a dynamic skin wheel with a keybind, hover over the skin you want, click, and you're done.

## Features and Usage

<details>
<summary>Skin wheel</summary>

Hold your keybind (default key is G) to open the overlay, hover over the skin you want, and **click** to apply it. You can **scroll** to explore the available wheels. Releasing the keybind without clicking simply closes the wheel and no skin will be changed.

<center>
  <img src="https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/kWMT8Yql/images/567726d300ff246714781878881ecea347ae3e60.gif" alt="Skin wheel">
</center>

You can reorganize the wheel by dragging the skin out, then dropping it where you want.

<center>
  <img src="https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/kWMT8Yql/images/9df9b7671aea4f763ecf0e464a0b9ec1c3fe12de.gif" alt="Skin wheel reorder">
</center>
</details>

<details>
<summary>Skin library screen</summary>

Allows you to add and organize your existing skins. You can open it from the main or pause menu. You can access it quickly by setting up the dedicated keybind as well.

<center>
  <img src="https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/kWMT8Yql/images/ff3424812519fa12db48b2b94f1d30a0f2d145dc.png" alt="Skin library screen">
</center>

You can:
- **Drag and drop** skins to **organize them in a category**
- **Drag and drop** skins to **add them to another category**
- **Create your own categories** and **configure** them to change the number of wheels they can have, their color, and name
- Quickly **add a skin** in the mod **from a Minecraft account** or **from an existing file** on your computer

</center>
<center>
  <img src="https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/kWMT8Yql/images/521383b6a44372d78211ad63a65316f4eb00b656.gif" alt="Skin drag and drop in category">

</details>

<details>
<summary>Multiplayer and servers</summary>

Skin switching also works in multiplayer and has all the other players see your skin change in real time! Three ways to make it work:
- **Server side mod**: You can add this mod to your Fabric server
- **SkinShuffleBridge plugin**: SimpleSkinSwapper is compatible with the SkinShuffleBridge plugin allowing it to work with paper servers
- **Server side skin update command**: If your server has a command to update your skin, you can configure it too

In any case, other players **DON'T** need to have the mod to see your skin change.

</details>
